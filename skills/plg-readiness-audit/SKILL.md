# PLG Readiness Audit

Most failed free trials weren't launched badly. They were launched before the business was ready. This skill answers one question with a committed verdict: **should this company launch (or keep running) a self-serve motion right now?**

## How to work

1. **Get the basics** (one message, 2–3 questions max): what they sell and to whom, price point, current motion and rough ARR, and what triggered the question.
2. **Run the Product-Led Test**: read `references/Product-Led Test.md`. Value Test: can a stranger experience real value without talking to a human? Economics Test: can they afford to give that proof away? Use `references/Cost of Proof.md` and `references/Proof Experience.md` to pressure-test the economics.
3. **Run MOAT**: read `references/MOAT Framework.md`. Flag each dimension 🟢/🟡/🔴: Market strategy, Ocean conditions, Adoption ease, Touch-to-value. Remember: pure-enterprise market is the only structural red flag; the rest are fixable.
4. **Run the Preflight Checklist**: read the Preflight Checklist file. Score the 5 Cs (Conviction, Commitment, Capabilities, Construction, Cash) and name the weakest one.

## Output format

1. **Verdict**: go, no-go, or not-yet, in one sentence with the single biggest reason.
2. **Product-Led Test results**: each test pass/fail with evidence. If failing, name the failure mode (lead magnet pretending to be PLG / generous freemium bleeding cash / free trial no one can activate).
3. **MOAT flags**: the four dimensions with flags and one line each.
4. **Weakest C**: from the Preflight Checklist, and what fixing it looks like.
5. **The one move**: the single highest-leverage action before launch, with a timeframe.

Never hedge into "it depends." Commit at ~70% information and state what evidence would change the verdict.
