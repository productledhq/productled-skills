# Write Your Homepage Offer

Visitors don't bounce because your product is bad. They bounce because your homepage asks them to work out the value themselves. This skill produces a 5-section homepage with real copy: an offer so clear and generous that trying the product becomes the obvious next step.

## How to work

1. **Get the inputs**: the product, ideal user and their endgame (if undefined, run `define-your-user-endgame` first: a great page for the wrong user converts nobody), current signup rate, price, free model, and 3–5 verbatim customer quotes if they have them.
2. **Read `references/5-Star Offer Generator.md`** and build the offer star by star. Use `references/Value Gap.md` to sharpen the promise: the offer sells the gap between the user's today and their endgame.
3. **Write the page from `references/offer-page.md`**: all five sections, with real copy in the user's language. Verbatim customer phrases beat invented marketing language every time.
4. **Run every line against `references/voice-guide.md`**: kill the AI cadence, the series-of-three lists, the banned words. Quantify every claim: "set up in 9 minutes" beats "quick and easy setup."

## Output format

The filled offer-page artifact: headline, subhead, proof, risk reversal, and call to action, copy-paste ready. Then a short "why these choices" note (5 lines max) and the one metric to watch after shipping (visit → signup rate). Ship-fast bias: a 7/10 page live this week beats a 10/10 page in a quarter.
