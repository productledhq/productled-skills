# Value Gap

> **Perceived value** (what we promise in marketing) − **Experienced value** (what we deliver in the product) = **Value Gap**.

## The Math

The bigger the Value Gap, the leakier the funnel.

- 40–60% of users who sign up for a product will use it once and never come back.
- A big Value Gap makes that worse.
- **You're not just losing users. You're teaching them not to trust you.**

## The Spaghetti Restaurant

Three versions:

| Restaurant | Experience | What it represents |
|---|---|---|
| **Kitchen-and-leave** | Walk in expecting spaghetti. Get raw ingredients and pointed at the stove. | Big Value Gap: you came for a meal, now you have to cook it |
| **Forgettable** | Food in 30–40 min. Fine. Nothing special. | Meeting expectations. 5/10. **Forgettable.** |
| **Michelin** | Same expectation. Extraordinary food, arrives fast, price feels like theft. | **Overdelivering.** You tell everyone. |

## The Three Levers of Overdelivery

Three things make a product overdeliver:
1. **Quality** of what the user gets
2. **Speed** to get it ([[Time-to-Value]])
3. **Price**

Nail one → competitive. Nail all three → people can't stop talking about you.

## Canva: All Three Nailed

| Before Canva | With Canva |
|---|---|
| Open Photoshop | Drag-and-drop |
| Watch tutorials | Beautiful templates make anyone look like a designer |
| Hours per design | Minutes |
| $600 piece of software | Free |

> "No wonder Canva didn't need to spend on advertising to grow initially. **Their users did the selling for them.**"

That's what happens when all three levers are pulled.

## The Speed Bar Keeps Moving

| Year | Standard for time-to-value |
|---|---|
| 2019 (1st edition) | An hour or two |
| 2026 | **<60 seconds** |

ChatGPT delivers in seconds. Lovable builds a working app in <1 minute.

> Your users aren't comparing your product to your competitors'. **They're comparing it to the fastest product they've ever used.**

## Self-Test

> Sign up for your own product as a new user and **count the seconds** until you hit a meaningful outcome.

| Time to meaningful outcome | Diagnosis |
|---|---|
| <60s | On the new bar |
| 1–5 min | Behind |
| 5+ min | Losing the majority of signups before they ever see what your product can do |
