# Audit Your Onboarding

The book's baseline: 40–60% of signups use the product once and never return. Onboarding isn't a product tour. It's a bowling lane: a straight line to first value, with bumpers that catch users when they drift. This skill maps the lane and produces a kill list.

## How to work

1. **Get the funnel**: monthly signups → completed setup → First Strike (first experience of core value) → repeat usage. If they can't provide these numbers, that's finding #1. You can't fix what you can't see.
2. **Define First Strike precisely** (`references/First Strike.md`): the moment the user first experiences the core value, stated as an observable event. Then check the straight line: every step between signup and First Strike either moves the user toward it or gets killed.
3. **Read `references/Bowling Alley Framework.md`** and map their current flow: the straight line, the product bumpers (in-app guidance), the conversational bumpers (email/messages that catch drift).
4. **Measure against time-to-value** (`references/Time-to-Value.md`): get the actual number in minutes/days, then set the target.
5. **Produce the audit from `references/onboarding-audit.md`**: the deliverable is the filled artifact with the kill list, not general advice.

## Output format

The completed onboarding audit: current funnel numbers, First Strike definition, the mapped lane, the **kill list** (every step that doesn't serve the straight line, marked for deletion), the missing bumpers, and the single highest-leverage fix with its expected metric impact.
