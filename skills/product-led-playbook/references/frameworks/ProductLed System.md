# The ProductLed System™

> A **GTM Operating System** for your business. The roadmap to scaling a product-led business and becoming the **[[Obvious Choice]]** in your market.

> **This is the spine of [[The Product-Led Playbook|Book 02]] of [[ProductLed Library]].** Book 01 introduces it via a preview. Book 02 is the full implementation guide, one chapter per component.

Nine components, organized into three stages. **80/20 Pareto:** 80% of results come from 20% of effort. These are the 20%.

Built over **10,000+ hours** of planning, teaching, coaching, and client work with **400+ companies generating $1B+ in self-serve revenue.** A living, breathing framework, not a one-time fix. Run a full cycle **at least once a year**, up to four times for fast-growing companies.

## Three Core Outcomes

1. **[[Effortless ARR]]**: product sells itself; users sign up, get value, upgrade without talking to anyone
2. **[[Lean Scale]]**: substantial revenue with a tiny team; high revenue-per-employee
3. **[[Durable Growth]]**: more profit every year; LTV increases with frequency of use

## Why It's an "Operating System"

Just like your computer runs Windows or macOS, your company has an operating system. **The minute you update any component, it starts to decay.**

- Your strategy is most relevant the day it's created
- Your offer is most unique the day it's crafted
- Your pricing is most powerful the day it's launched

> To stay at the cutting edge of your market, you must constantly update each component. **At minimum, one full cycle per year.** Fast-growing companies: 2–4 cycles per year.

Common feedback from first-time implementers: *"I'm working less but have way more impact."* From frenetic → focused.

## Why the Order Matters

> Optimizing onboarding without a clear offer is futile. Scaling your team without customer traction is a drain on resources.

| Skip | Symptom |
|---|---|
| Strategy | Every team rows a different direction |
| User | Every other component is built for a phantom |
| Model | Great offer but no path to value |
| Offer | Great onboarding but no signups |
| Onboarding | Signups don't convert |
| Pricing | Signups convert but don't expand |
| Data | Can't see the bottleneck |
| Process | See the bottleneck but never fix it |
| Team | None of the above compounds |

## Three Common Pitfalls This System Addresses

1. **Approaching PLG as a "product" thing.** It's GTM, not product strategy. *(80% of ProductLed's results came from the 20% of clients who were founders implementing PLG company-wide.)*
2. **Not providing meaningful, timely value before monetizing.** 40–60% of signups never come back.
3. **Not executing effectively.** Wrong order = wasted quarters.

## The Surface vs. Below-the-Surface Truth

> [[Surface-Level PLG]] = doing only Stage 2 (the PLG motion users touch). [[Product-Led Organization (PLO)|PLO]] = Stages 1 and 3 (the back-end structure).

**Becoming product-led = integrating both.**

The visible PLG motion is just Stage 2 (Offer · Onboarding · Pricing). **Stages 1 and 3 are what build the [[Product-Led Organization (PLO)|PLO]] underneath**: the back-end structure that makes the PLG motion actually work.

> Many companies deploy PLG (Stage 2 only) without building a PLO (Stages 1 and 3). *That's what happened at Vidyard.*
