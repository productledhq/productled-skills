# Predictable Growth Process

> **Execution makes you the best, even if you're not the first.** Apple didn't invent the portable music player. Google wasn't the first search engine. Tesla didn't make the first EV. **They executed.**

## The Three Meeting Cadences

```
QUARTERLY  ─────►   Strategic Alignment Meeting (SAM)
                   Day-long. Refines strategy. Sets the quarter.
                          │
MONTHLY    ─────►   Monthly Focus Meeting (MFM)
                   Half-day. Strategy → action. 80% of effort → 1 focus.
                          │
WEEKLY     ─────►   Weekly Accelerator Meeting (WAM)
                   90 min. Momentum + accountability.
```

> **Don't facilitate SAM and MFM yourself.** Use a ProductLed Implementer (or external facilitator). When planning the quarter, you need to be 100% on the discussion, not managing the process.

## Strategic Alignment Meeting (SAM): Quarterly

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Who | Leadership + dept heads (Product · Eng · Marketing · Sales), ≤10 people |
| Facilitator | ProductLed Implementer |
| Duration | Full day |
| Frequency | Quarterly *(replaces MFM that month)* |
| Prep | Review [[One-Page Endgame (OPE)\|OPE]] · Update [[ProductLed Scorecard]] · List strategic priorities + issues |

### Agenda (~6 hours)

| Time | Item | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 15 min | **Check-in** | Everyone's expectations for the session |
| 30 min | **Retro** | Past quarter: went well / didn't / to do differently |
| 30 min | **Strategy Review** | Walk the [[One-Page Endgame (OPE)\|OPE]] section by section |
| 30 min | **Accountability Chart Review** | Who owns what; gaps |
| 15 min | **Scorecard Review** | Each owner marks status (see below) |
| 10 min | **Targets** | For upcoming quarter |
| 20 min | **Top Goals** | 3–5 SMART quarterly goals |
| **90 min** | **Issues** | The heart of the meeting: discuss + plan |
| 30 min | **Component Deep Dive** | Refresh one of the 9 components |
| 30 min | **Top Focus** | For the upcoming month |
| 30 min | **Top Projects** | For the upcoming month, with owners |
| 5 min | **Cascading Messages** | All-hands or email recap |
| 5 min | **Conclude** | Rate meeting + feedback |

### The Five-Status System (Scorecard Review)

Each owner marks every metric:
1. **On Track**
2. **Trending On Track**
3. **Off Track, Have a Plan** *(must link the plan, create an issue, review it)*
4. **Off Track, No Plan** → goes to Issues
5. **Off Track, No Plan, Target Unreachable** → goes to Issues

> Great leaders showcase ownership by creating a plan of attack when their metric is off. **Peer-to-peer accountability at the highest level.**

### Component Deep Dive

Each SAM picks one component to refresh:
- 12 months since updating strategy? → [[01 - Strategy Component|Strategy]]
- Lacking clarity on ideal user? → [[02 - User Component|User]]
- Lots of traffic but few signups? → [[04 - Offer Component|Offer]]

> The [[ProductLed System]] is not set-it-and-forget-it. Each component is a plant: water it, maintain it, reap fruit.

## Monthly Focus Meeting (MFM)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Who | Leadership team |
| Facilitator | ProductLed Implementer |
| Duration | Half-day |
| Frequency | Monthly |
| Prep | Update scorecard · List projects & issues |

> Planning is a muscle. MFMs give leadership time to work **on** the business, not **in** it.

### Agenda

| Time | Item |
|---|---|
| 15 min | **Retro**: 3 questions (well / didn't / differently) |
| 30 min | **Top Focus & Project Review**: were last month's projects done? *(Off-track if not done by month-end.)* |
| 15 min | **Scorecard Review** |
| 10 min | **Targets**: update OPE Monthly Picture |
| **45 min** | **Issues** |
| 10 min | **Top Focus** for next month |
| 20 min | **Top Projects** for next month |
| 5 min | **Cascading Messages** |
| 5 min | **Conclude** |

### Prioritizing Projects

Two questions:
1. Which projects have biggest impact on our Top Focus?
2. Which projects best help us hit our Targets?

For each project, define:
- Who owns it?
- Who's responsible for executing each aspect?
- What's involved with rolling it out?

> Max **5 top projects per person.** Let the team know it's okay to pivot or kill projects that become irrelevant.

## Weekly Accelerator Meeting (WAM)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Who | Leadership team |
| Duration | 90 min |
| Frequency | Weekly |
| Prep | Update scorecard + project progress |

### Agenda

| Time | Item |
|---|---|
| 5 min | **Wins**: one personal + one professional |
| 3 min | **Monthly Picture Review**: on/off track |
| 3 min | **Scorecard Review**: on/off track per owner |
| 3 min | **Project Review**: on/off track |
| 3 min | **To-Do Review**: from last week |
| **60 min** | **Issues**: root-cause + resolve |
| 10 min | **Top 3 Priorities + To-Dos**: per person |
| 3 min | **Rating** + feedback |

> Top 3 priorities review increases collaboration + peer accountability. Captured in your PM tool for next week's reference.

## Why Speed Wins

> In today's markets, **it's not the big that eats the small. It's the fast that eats the slow.** Drag your feet → get copied.

Aligning quarterly on the vision + locking key projects monthly + making massive progress weekly = an **unstoppable rhythm.**

> **Speed is the ultimate long-term competitive advantage.**

## Meeting Pulse Discipline

- Tough times → pulse faster
- Smooth sailing → slow the pulse for longer initiatives
- SAMs are the **most important meetings in the company**: they shape the future
- MFMs keep 80% of work aligned to the top priority
- WAMs unstick hairy issues mid-week
