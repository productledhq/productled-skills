# 5-Star Offer Generator

> Within seconds of landing on your homepage, users drop into one of three buckets:
> 1. You can't help me
> 2. You might be able to help me
> 3. You can absolutely help me: this is the best solution

The bar to stay in **bucket 3** = your offer must **stand the f\*ck out** (Louis Grenier).

> **Secret:** If your strategy sucks, so will your offer. If your strategy is rock solid, your offer will be too.

## Three Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Being Cute
"We're the AI-enabled solution to your biggest challenges." So you do... what?

### Mistake 2: No Enhancers
Chocolate ice cream is delicious. Add peanut butter → 10/10. **Without enhancers, you won't be irresistible.**

### Mistake 3: Lack of Structure
A good offer page flows like a story: meet villain → sign up → beat villain. Most pages just ramble.

## Three Phases

```
PHASE 1 ──► Core Offer
              Result + Advantage + Assurance
                     │
PHASE 2 ──► Enhancers
              Exclusivity + Bonuses
                     │
PHASE 3 ──► Assemble (5-Section Homepage)
              Hero · Problem · Solution · Risk Reversal · CTA
```

## Phase 1: Core Offer (3 Pillars)

### Pillar 1: The Result
Tangible, measurable outcome. **Anything not directly attached to an outcome isn't specific enough.**

| ❌ Vague | ✅ Specific |
|---|---|
| "Better, faster, grow your business" | "Designed for agencies using WordPress" |
| | "Flat-rate pricing" |

> Pull from the top 3 outcomes you defined in the [[02 - User Component|User Component]].

**PromoTix results:**
- Help event organizers be more profitable
- Drive more sales with marketing tools
- Start selling quickly by publishing your event

### Pillar 2: The Advantage
Why your product is **5–10× better** than alternatives.

- Zoom: audio filter blocks background noise (use it in coffee shops)
- Canva: far easier than Photoshop
- **PromoTix: up to 40% cheaper than Eventbrite**

Run each through the **"Why does it matter?" test.** If everyone in your space is hard to use, "easier" matters. If not → not the lead.

> If stuck, go back to your "how to win" strategy. **Your advantages should reinforce your [[15 Moats|moats]].**

### Pillar 3: The Assurance
Address fears directly.

**Common objections:** ROI · Setup & Training · Support · Collaboration · Security

**Common assurances:**
- Money-Back Guarantee
- Price Match Guarantee
- Satisfaction Guarantee
- Onboarding & Training (free or discounted)
- Customer Support Guarantee (specific response/resolution times)
- Free Trials
- Social Proof (awards, logos)

> **Quantify everything.** Don't say "easy setup", say "average 12-min setup based on past customer data."

**PromoTix:**

| Objection | Assurance |
|---|---|
| Upfront cost to set up the event | No credit card required to start |
| Doesn't work for big AND small events | Complete attendee management |
| I won't be able to sell many tickets | Sell 22% more tickets with our marketing tools |

### Why All Three Are Needed

- Only Result + Assurance → why use *this* solution?
- Only Result + Advantage → what are the risks?
- Only Assurance + Advantage → what are the results?

## Phase 2: Enhancers

Takes 7/10 → 10/10. **Up to +15% conversions.**

### Exclusivity
Drives scarcity + urgency.

**Scarcity**: what will break if you add too many customers? Artificial scarcity doesn't work; authentic does.

**Urgency forms:**
1. Limited-time offer
2. Exploding opportunity ("PLG is hot. If you don't, a competitor will.")
3. Cohort-based urgency (firm enrollment deadline)
4. Bonus-based urgency (free gift expires)

### Bonuses
Random bonuses don't excite ("open a bank account, get a toaster").

**Good bonuses tie back to the User and Model Components**: address challenges not yet solved.

> Example: user onboarding software → bonus = free user onboarding course (valued at $999, free this month).

**Keap example:** Small business owners rarely automate processes (Keap's core differentiator). So they gave away a **free audit** to identify one crucial process + set it up. **Skyrocketed retention.**

**Types:** free gifts · free resources (assessments, training) · 1:1 expert support · free audits.

> Pick 3 enhancers. You don't have to use them all.

## Phase 3: Assemble Your 5-Section Homepage

```
┌─────────────────────┐
│  1. HERO SECTION    │  ← grab attention; main value prop
├─────────────────────┤
│  2. PROBLEM SECTION │  ← build interest; spiky POV
├─────────────────────┤
│  3. SOLUTION SECTION│  ← "how does it work?" + result
├─────────────────────┤
│  4. RISK REVERSAL   │  ← objections + social proof
├─────────────────────┤
│  5. CALL TO ACTION  │  ← clear next step
└─────────────────────┘
```

### 1. Hero Section
Five pieces:
- **Tagline**: communicates the Result
- **Sub-copy**: reinforces what you do + prompts to start
- **CTA**: simple, action-first prompt
- **Visual** (optional): quickly communicates what words can't
- **Social proof**: reputable signal

**PromoTix before → after (yielded +40% signups, same traffic):**

| | Old | New |
|---|---|---|
| Value Prop | Built for event creators | **Sell more tickets without insanely high ticketing fees.** |
| Subtitle | Low fees. Powerful marketing. | Finally, a way to market and sell more tickets without needing a ton of other marketing tools and without high service fees. Get up and selling in under 5 minutes. |
| CTA | Sign Up | **Ticket My Event** |
| Social Proof | Customer logos | Awards from Capterra, SourceForge, Slashdot |

### 2. Problem Section
Wes Kao (Maven): your **"spiky point of view."** A tad controversial. Not everyone agrees. Plays into your underlying differentiator.

- ProductLed: *"Product-led growth is more than a free trial."*
- PromoTix: *"Ticketmaster, eTix, Eventbrite, and others have service fees ranging from 20 to 40%, and the fees are climbing..."*

Structure: **Spiky POV → Top Challenges → Agreeable Viewpoint.**

> Don't shy away from confrontation. PromoTix highlighted that the DOJ was suing competitors for high fees.

> Test: write it as a LinkedIn post. **Falls flat → not spiky enough.** Drives engagement → onto something.

### 3. Solution Section
"How does it work?" + reinforces the underlying result.

> **Ideally ≤5 steps.** Don't have to be sequential.

Make users feel they can achieve the result by reading the copy alone. **Give a taste of what solving the problem feels like.**

### 4. Risk Reversal Section
List every reason your team has heard for non-signups. Patterns emerge.

Common: What's my ROI? · Setup time? · Works with other tools?

**Tools:**
- Guarantees
- Endorsements from influential people
- User reviews & ratings (G2, Trustpilot)
- Client logos
- Media coverage
- User count
- Demo version
- Certifications

> Deploy social proof **alongside every section**, not just here. Expand into a before/after customer story.

### 5. Call-to-Action Section
- Why sign up now?
- What can they do once they sign up?
- Is it risk-free?
- What's the next step?

> Lean into top reasons. **Don't introduce new content.**

## Ship Fast, Iterate

> Set an aggressive deadline. Even if it doesn't feel "perfect," launch.

PromoTix A/B tested first → 40% signup lift in 1 week → rolled out to 100%.

> Too many companies spend months refining internally when they could get real user feedback.
