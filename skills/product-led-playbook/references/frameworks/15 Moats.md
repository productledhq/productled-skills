# 15 Moats

> A **moat** = true business defensibility. Like the ancient moat around Chiang Mai: attackers must swim *and* climb the walls.

> You can and should build more than one. **Ideally three.** A competitor might copy one moat, but copying three is nearly impossible.

## The 15 Moats

### 1. Differentiation Moat
Unique product/service that fulfills needs in a distinct and valuable way. Innovative features, superior quality, niche focus, or a combination.

### 2. Cost Leadership Moat
Become the low-cost provider while maintaining acceptable quality. Economies of scale + efficient operations.

### 3. User Experience Moat
Seamless, intuitive UX extended throughout the customer journey. Personalized interactions, long-term loyalty.

### 4. Network Effects Moat
Value increases as more people use it. Meta, social platforms. **Webflow, WordPress, Miro, Canva**: armies of users create templates for others.

### 5. Operational Efficiency Moat
Streamlined internal processes + optimized resource allocation. Better profitability, faster delivery, satisfaction.

### 6. Branding and Reputation Moat
Strong brand + positive reputation = loyalty, trust, premium pricing.

### 7. Distribution Moat
Extensive network or exclusive access to key channels, strategic partnerships, alliances.

> Early apps in Shopify or Android app stores get the lion's share of downloads + reviews → hard to replicate. ProductLed benefited the same way: early PLG content + backlinks + authority before others were even talking about PLG.

### 8. Speed and Innovation Moat
> *It's not the big that eats the small. It's the fast that eats the slow.* (Jennings & Haughton.)

One of the most underrated. Especially powerful when breaking into a stagnant industry.

### 9. Pricing Moat
A unique pricing strategy that aligns with customer success.

Examples: free, covered by sponsorships · % per transaction (charge only when users get value) · **Basecamp and Flodesk** don't charge for additional users while most competitors do.

### 10. Free Moat
Beyond just a free product experience, **subsidize adjacent free products with your core product.** Expand into adjacent markets, capture demand for your other products.

> **ConvertKit** (email marketing) launched a free landing page tool to make it easier for creators to capture signups.

### 11. Switching Cost Moat
High switching costs via contracts, integrations, data lock-in.

> **Alina Vandenberghe (Chili Piper)** reduced churn by **35%** for orgs with 3+ integrations.

### 12. Proprietary Moat
Patents, trademarks, copyrights, trade secrets.

> ProductLed developed the **ProductLed System™** as a proprietary process.

### 13. Engagement Moat
Vibrant brand community, active user engagement, user-generated content → strong word-of-mouth and loyalty.

### 14. Expansion Moat
Make the most per customer in your industry, through multiple products bought over time, revenue model + high loyalty, or LTV growth.

> **HubSpot**: start with free CRM → Marketing hub → Sales hub → Service hub. **One $50/mo customer eventually becomes $5,000/mo.**

### 15. Founder Brand Moat
Hard to copy. Do you want to do business with a faceless competitor or a company where you feel like you know the founder?

> Adam Robinson (RB2B) · Dave Gerhardt (Exit Five) · Guillaume Moubeche (Lemlist) · Jason Fried (37signals) · Olly Meakings (Senja).

## Four Common Combinations

| Strategy | Moat combo |
|---|---|
| **Low-cost leader** (Southwest) | Cost Leadership + Operational Efficiency |
| **Go upmarket** | Differentiation + Customer Experience + Switching Costs |
| **Dominant market leader** | Cost Leadership + Operational Efficiency + UX |
| **Disrupt stagnant industry** (Canva vs. Photoshop) | Free Moat + Speed + Cost Leadership |

> Individually, moats aren't that special. Together they create an unstoppable combination.

## Building Discipline

- Don't build too many simultaneously. **Start with 1–2.** Once built, add another.
- **Maintain them.** Set-and-forget = moat evaporates. *Example: Differentiation Moat with unique features → competitor copies → users see fewer differences.*

## Sample (Paubox)

| Moat | Reason |
|---|---|
| Differentiation | Method of sending encrypted emails has patented IP |
| User & Customer Experience | Ideal users value an easy-to-use solution: want to keep their existing email but be HIPAA compliant |
| Customer Support | Ideal users aren't technical; need help integrating with Google Workspace / Outlook |
