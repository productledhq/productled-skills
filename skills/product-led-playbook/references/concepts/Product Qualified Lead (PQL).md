# Product Qualified Lead (PQL)

> An ideal user who has successfully set up their account, received core value, and hit a [[Key Usage Indicator (KUI)|KUI]]. **30–50% odds of upgrading.**

> Replaces the **MQL (Marketing Qualified Lead)** for product-led companies. MQLs treat downloading an ebook as a buying signal. **PQLs treat actual product usage as the signal.**

## The Formula

> **PQL = Ideal User Profile + Successful Setup + [[First Strike]] + Hit a [[Key Usage Indicator (KUI)|KUI]]**

Each ingredient:
1. **Ideal User Profile**: they match your ideal (asked via profiling questions at signup)
2. **Successful Setup**: they completed the setup stage (e.g., installed a script)
3. **First Strike**: they experienced tangible value
4. **Hit a KUI**: they're forming a habit; experiencing value frequently

## Why It Matters

> An advanced metric. **Don't add it until you've been using the [[ProductLed Scorecard]] for at least a month.**

When used correctly, PQLs:
- **Incentivize marketing** to drive *ideal* signups (not just signups)
- **Simplify customer success**: who to assist first
- **Clarify sales**: who's ready to upgrade

> Every team can play a part. **PQLs align your team's success with your user's endgame.**

## How to Roll Out PQLs

Modify the six GTM metrics from the [[True North Framework]]:

| Without PQLs | With PQLs |
|---|---|
| Unique visits | Unique visits |
| Signups | **Ideal Signups** |
| Setup | Setup |
| First Strike | First Strike |
| KUI | **PQLs** |
| Upgrade | **Ideal users who upgrade** |

The two segmenting moves:
- "Signups" → "**Ideal** Signups"
- "KUI" → "PQL" (adds the Ideal User segment)
- "Upgrade" → "**Ideal users** who upgrade"

## Why the Ideal User Filter Matters

> Without segmentation, "mediocre" conversion might actually be great if you looked only at ideal users. "Amazing" campaigns might have brought in scores of bad-fit users. **Unsegmented data is a mixed bag.**

## Connection to Sales-Led Critique

In Book 01, Wes critiques the MQL: 98% of MQLs never convert (SiriusDecisions). The MQL machine encourages marketers to **gate content and treat lead-magnet downloads as buying signals.**

> Downloading an ebook is **not** a buying signal. **Hitting a KUI inside your product is.**

The PQL is the product-led replacement for the MQL.
