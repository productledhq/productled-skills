# Lean Scale

> Generate substantial revenue with a tiny team. **Your product does the heavy lifting.**

One of the three outcomes of the [[ProductLed System]]:
1. [[Effortless ARR]]
2. **Lean Scale** ← this note
3. [[Durable Growth]]

## The Metric: Revenue per Employee (RPE)

> Total revenue ÷ number of full-time employees.

If revenue is $1M with 10 FTEs → RPE = $100,000.

**High RPE** = efficient, product does heavy lifting.
**Low RPE** = overly reliant on people.

## The Benchmark Levels

| Level | RPE Range | State |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | $0–$100K | Treading Water |
| L2 | $100K–$300K | Streamlined Company |
| L3 | $300K–$700K | Prolific Performers |
| L4 | $1M–$2M+ | **Ace Team** |

L4+ → Wes recommends targeting **$3–5M RPE.**

> **Only 4% of companies ever enjoy an RPE of $1M+.**

## The Leverage Line

> Everything above the "Leverage Line" (~L3) means your **team is the machine.**
> Everything below means your **team is the machine**, and growth demands more resources + manual effort.

Companies without leverage are trapped in a cycle of overwork and inefficiency. Instead of effortless scale, growth demands more resources and manual effort. Stifles innovation. Consumes bandwidth.

> Shift focus from **scaling through people** to **scaling through products.**

> **Your product should be your best employee, salesperson, and customer service rep.**
> It drives revenue, attracts customers, scales effortlessly. Your lean team focuses on **steering the boat, not rowing it.**

## Real-World Examples

| Company | Revenue | Headcount | RPE |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Userflow** | $5M ARR | 3 | **$1.67M** |
| **Midjourney** | $200M | <40 | >$5M |
| **Genspark AI** | $200M+ | 50 | $4M+ |

> Userflow sold for an **8× revenue multiple** after ~3 years.

## How the [[ProductLed System]] Drives Lean Scale

- **Stage 1 (Foundation):** Strategy + User + Model → right users, right offer, right model
- **Stage 2 (Self-Serve):** Offer + Onboarding + Pricing → users sign up, get value, upgrade without your team
- **Stage 3 (Expansion):** Data + Process + Team → product handles execution while a lean team steers

> In a product-led business, your product is the machine, **not your team.** That's what gives you leverage and maintains lean scale.

## Hiring Implication

> When you've nailed the prior components, you can afford to **hire as a last resort.** Your product solves most problems.

The [[Elite Team Flywheel]] explicitly uses the 3T system (Trash/Transfer/Treasure) to eliminate 10–20% of jobs that aren't required.
