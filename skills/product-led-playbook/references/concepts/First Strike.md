# First Strike

> The **first time** a user experiences the core value of your product.

The First Strike is one of two milestones in the **Showcase Stage** of the [[User Endgame Roadmap]]. The other is the [[Key Usage Indicator (KUI)]].

## What It's NOT

> **Not the entire value of the product.** Not "they've upgraded." Not "they're a power user."

> Enough value to motivate them to come back.

## Examples

| Product | First Strike |
|---|---|
| Canva | Creating your first graphic |
| Zoom | Having your first call |
| Netflix | Watching your first movie |
| Chess.com | Playing your first game |

## Why It Matters

> 40–60% of first-time users sign up and never come back. **Most of that drop happens before First Strike.**

That's why when you first build a [[Bowling Alley Framework|straight line]], you map only from Visit → First Strike. The biggest impact lives there.

## The Goal

> A great First Strike delivers significant value **while leaving room for further discovery.**

Mario picks up the flower → grows bigger, can spew fire. Significant new capability, but Mario still has a long game to play.

## Relationship to KUI

| | First Strike | [[Key Usage Indicator (KUI)\|KUI]] |
|---|---|---|
| What | First time experiencing core value | Multiple times experiencing it (habit forming) |
| Frequency | Once | Often = 3+ First Strikes |
| Signal | "I see what this does" | "I'm coming back for more" |
| Predicts | Returning | Upgrading |
