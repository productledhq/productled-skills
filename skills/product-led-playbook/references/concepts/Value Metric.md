# Value Metric

> **The way you measure value exchange in your product.** The linchpin of a product-led GTM strategy.

> Patrick Campbell (former ProfitWell CEO): **value metrics outperform feature differentiation with up to 75% less churn.**

## The Core Idea

You're aligning your **revenue model** with the **success of your user.** When they win, you win.

## Examples

| Company | Value Metric |
|---|---|
| Vidyard | # of videos uploaded |
| Slack | # of users |
| Paddle | Total revenue generated (% per transaction) |
| Email marketing | # of contacts |
| Practice management | # of appointments booked |

## Two Questions a Value Metric Answers

1. **What does our ideal customer want** in a product or service?
2. **How much is the ideal customer willing and able to pay?**

## Criteria for a Great Value Metric

1. **Simple to understand**: when someone visits the pricing page, they immediately know what they're paying for
2. **Aligned with the product's core value**: often = your [[Key Usage Indicator (KUI)|KUI]]
3. **Scalable**: fair to charge more at higher usage

> Charge more when customers get more value. Charge less if they aren't getting full value.

## The Senja Example

Should Senja's value metric be number of users?

| Metric | Simple? | Aligned? | Scalable? |
|---|---|---|---|
| # of users | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| # of testimonials collected | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |

If Senja uses user-based pricing, value and pricing are misaligned. With testimonials collected, **as customers collect more testimonials, Senja can charge more.** Aligned.

## How to Find Yours

1. **Hypothesize.** Messages sent? Number of users? Revenue generated?
2. **Market scan** (if in an established market; Greg Leach's advice). The metric is likely well-defined; benefit from convention.
3. **Review your [[DEEP Model]].** One of the easiest ways to spot a value metric: which items remain the same across all packages but have **different numerical amounts**?
4. **Run the Value Metrics Scratchpad.** Score each candidate on Simple / Aligned / Scalable.

> Limit to **1–2 value metrics.** Less is more.

## Trap to Avoid: Misaligned Incentives

If you charge by features and a Walmart-sized customer signs up at $99/mo and processes $100M in month one, your team is buried. Either cancel the customer or repackage.

> Paddle's solution: charge **3–5% of revenue processed** (like a credit card). Scales with the value customers receive.
