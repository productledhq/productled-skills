# One-Page Endgame (OPE)

> A **single-page distillation of your entire strategy**, the deliverable of the [[Bullseye Strategy Framework]].

## What Goes On It

Everything you decide across the five Bullseye steps:

### From Step 1: Obvious Choice Statement
*"We are the obvious choice for [SEGMENT] who struggle with [PROBLEM]."*

### From Step 2: Where to Play
- Ideal Customer
- Main Problem
- Core Product
- Primary Marketing Channels
- Geography

### From Step 3: How to Win
- Your [[15 Moats|moats]] (ideally 3) and the reason for each

### From Step 4: Winning Picture
- **Endgame** (5–10 years out, SMART)
- **1-Year Picture**: Top Outcomes (revenue/RPE/profit) + 3 Annual Goals + Reward
- **Quarterly Picture**: Top Outcomes + 3 Quarterly Goals + Reward
- **Monthly Picture**: Top Focus + Top Monthly Projects + Reward

### From Step 5: Strategic Choices
- Core Values (aim for 3)
- Core Capabilities (new / double-down / no-longer-needed)
- Supplemental Strategic Choices (what to say NO to)

## Who Should See It

Share with everyone who:
- Has to **act** on it
- Will be **affected** by changes
- Could offer **valuable feedback**

Explain how you arrived at the strategy and how the business will change.

## How It's Used

### In meetings
- Reviewed **section by section** at every [[Predictable Growth Process|SAM (quarterly)]]
- The **Monthly Picture** is updated at every [[Predictable Growth Process|MFM]]
- Reviewed for on-track status at every [[Predictable Growth Process|WAM]]

### In the [[09 - Team Component|Team]] Component
- **Core values** become the basis of the Quick Audit
- **Capabilities** drive the Accountability Chart structure

## Strategy as a Living Document

> The first few times you review your OPE, you might change your moats, clarify your ideal user, and nail down some strategic choices. **This is completely normal.**

> The OPE is a **living document** that will evolve as your company scales.

## The CEO's Job

> Jason Fried (37signals): *"The CEO's main job is to introduce risk into the business. Everyone else's job is to manage and mitigate that risk."*

> Your strategy is nothing more than a theory on how you're going to win. **If you have 100% confidence, you waited too long.** Your competitors know it too.
