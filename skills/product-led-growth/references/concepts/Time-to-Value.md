# Time-to-Value (TTV)

> **The one metric that drives every era of PLG.**

Each version of PLG exists because the previous one was too slow:

| Era | Goal | TTV bar |
|---|---|---|
| [[PLG 1.0, 2.0, 3.0\|PLG 1.0]] | Shorten the path to value | Minutes–hours |
| PLG 2.0 | Collapse the path to value | <60 seconds |
| PLG 3.0 | Eliminate the path entirely | Before you ask |

## The Moving Bar

When the 1st edition of *Product-Led Growth* was written (2019), getting a user to value **within an hour or two was the standard**. By 2026:

- ChatGPT delivers value in seconds
- Lovable lets you build a working app in <1 minute
- **The new benchmark for PLG success is value in <60 seconds**

> Your users aren't comparing your product to your competitors'. **They're comparing it to the fastest product they've ever used.**

## When the Clock Actually Starts

> Most sales-led companies don't factor this gap into their time-to-value. The clock doesn't start when the demo happens. **It starts the moment a buyer decides to evaluate your product.**

The client portal example: "Book a Demo" → nine days to schedule → buyer found and started using an alternative. Nine days of dead time cost the company a customer.

## How to Measure

> **Time it.** Sign up for your own product as a new user and count seconds to a meaningful outcome.

| Result | Diagnosis |
|---|---|
| <60s | On the new bar |
| 1–5 min | Behind |
| 5+ min | Losing most signups before they see what the product does |

## The Three Eras' Approach to TTV

### PLG 1.0: shorten
- Better onboarding, fewer clicks, guided activation
- Tools: Pendo, Appcues, UserGuiding, Userflow, WalkMe
- Limit: the user still does all the work

### PLG 2.0: collapse
- Lead with an AI-generated output the user refines
- Activation = first touchpoint (not step 5)
- The homepage *is* the product

### PLG 3.0: eliminate
- The agent is the orchestrator
- Value shows up before you even ask for it
- Netlify's majority of new signups are now agents
