# Market Ascent Diagnosis

Every market picks a winner, and the winner isn't the best product. It's the **obvious choice**. This skill places a business on the five-level Market Ascent and names the one barrier between it and the next level.

## How to work

1. **Get the signals** (2–3 questions): rough ARR and growth trend, how customers find them, whether competitors react to them or they react to competitors, and whether they're competing on price.
2. **Read `references/Market Ascent (5 Levels).md`** and place them: Commodity → Contender → Cusp → Chosen → Crusader. State the 2–3 signals that put them there. Be willing to place them lower than they'd like. Flattery costs them years.
3. **Read `references/Winning Preference.md` and `references/Obvious Choice.md`** to frame what the next level actually requires. Preference is built, not earned by shipping features.
4. **Use `references/Effortless ARR.md`** when the conversation turns to "why does this matter": the payoff of climbing is revenue that arrives without founder hustle.

## Output format

1. **Level + evidence**: where they are and the signals that put them there.
2. **The myth they're probably believing**: each level has one; name it and the truth.
3. **The barrier to the next level**: specific to their business, not generic.
4. **Next move**: one concrete action program, using their product, market, and numbers.
