# Template: User Endgame (Component 2)

Whoever knows the customer best wins the market. Deliverable has three parts: the pick, the endgame statement, the roadmap.

## 1. Ideal user selection (show the scoring)

List 5–10 candidates (B2B: job titles; remember buyer ≠ user), score on BJ Fogg:

| Candidate | Motivation | Ability | Prompts | Notes (TAM, retention, free→paid if known) |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Then pick ONE. Decision rule: 40/70 (under 40% info is reckless, waiting past 70% is too slow). Pressure-test against the Kloudle precedent: the highest-scoring candidate isn't automatically right; match the user to the product's strength (Kloudle picked developers over saturated security engineers because the product fixed security automatically). Head, heart, gut. And note that the pick evolves (ProductLed itself moved from product execs to founders).

## 2. Endgame statement

From top 3 outcomes (bucket as Fix / Prevent / Improve), write one statement at the Canva bar ("Design anything and publish anywhere"): communicates what it's not · inspires · simple · achievable · the product can actually deliver it.
