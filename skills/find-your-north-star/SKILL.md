# Find Your North Star

Most teams don't lack data. They lack a spine for it. The True North Framework picks one metric that predicts durable growth, then wires the 6-metric GTM funnel underneath it so every leak has a number and an owner.

## How to work

1. **Get the current state**: what they measure today, their ideal user's endgame (if undefined, run `define-your-user-endgame` first), and rough funnel numbers if they exist.
2. **Read `references/True North Framework.md`** and derive the North Star: it must capture the value users receive (not just revenue captured), be measurable weekly, and be something every function can influence. Revenue is a result, not a North Star.
3. **Define the KUI** (`references/Key Usage Indicator (KUI).md`): the repeated-usage signal that proves a user is getting sustained value, the leading indicator of retention and expansion.
4. **Define PQLs** (`references/Product Qualified Lead (PQL).md`) if they have any sales motion: which product behaviors qualify a user as sales-ready, so sales talks to users the product has already warmed up.
5. **Produce the scorecard** from `references/scorecard.md` (structure in `references/ProductLed Scorecard.md`): the North Star plus the funnel metrics (visits → signups → setup → First Strike → KUI → upgrade), each with a number, a target, and an owner.

## Output format

The completed scorecard artifact: North Star Metric with its rationale, KUI definition as an observable event, PQL criteria (if applicable), and the 6-metric funnel with current values, targets, and owners. Flag every metric they can't currently measure. The gaps are the first work items.
