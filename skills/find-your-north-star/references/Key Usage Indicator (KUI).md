# Key Usage Indicator (KUI)

> The signal that a user is **forming a habit**: experiencing your product's core value multiple times.

The KUI is the second milestone of the **Showcase Stage** in the [[User Endgame Roadmap]]. The first is the [[First Strike]].

## Definition

> The true sign you've got a user hooked: **they come back for more.**

Often = experiencing the [[First Strike]] **three or more times.**

## The Peanut Butter Energy Ball

> Wes ate a peanut butter chocolate energy ball before breakfast. Five minutes after breakfast, he had another one. **That's a KUI**: clear sign he loves them.

Your product should produce the same effect. **First Strike = the first yummy energy ball. KUI = the second.**

## Canonical Examples

| Product | KUI |
|---|---|
| Slack | Account reaches 2,000 messages |
| Facebook | 7 friends added |

## Why It Matters

> A KUI is a **leading indicator** of who is getting meaningful value from your product, and who is **far more likely to upgrade.**

KUIs are the foundation of [[Product Qualified Lead (PQL)|PQLs]]:

> **PQL = Ideal User Profile + Successful Setup + [[First Strike]] + Hit a KUI**

## KUI vs. Value Metric vs. NSM

These are close cousins:

- **KUI** = the behavior signaling habit and likely upgrade
- **[[Value Metric]]** = what you base your pricing on
- **North Star Metric ([[True North Framework|NSM]])** = the company-wide alignment metric

Often, all three are the same underlying behavior. Slack's KUI of 2,000 messages → number of messages is also the pricing-relevant value metric → NSM is messages per week.
