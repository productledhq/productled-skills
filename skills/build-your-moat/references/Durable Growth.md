# Durable Growth

> Unlock **more profit every year**, like clockwork.

One of the three outcomes of the [[ProductLed System]]:
1. [[Effortless ARR]]
2. [[Lean Scale]]
3. **Durable Growth** ← this note

## Why Profit Matters

> A lack of profitability makes companies **fragile.** One bump in the road can be fatal.

But high profitability alone isn't invincible either. A CEO could harvest profits for short-term gains at the expense of marketing and product development.

> You want **durable growth: profits that increase every year**, requiring your business to evolve with the market.

## The Metric: Net Profit

| Level | Profit Range | State |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | $0–$100K | Tiny Profits |
| L2 | $100K–$1M | Small Reserve |
| L3 | $1M–$5M | Fair Fortune |
| L4 | $5M–$10M | **Automatic Profits** |

> Most companies start with small profits. Get to sizable profits **before** worrying about annual profit growth.

## At L4+: Net Profit Growth Rate

Formula: (this year's profit − last year's profit) ÷ last year's profit.

E.g., $500K → $600K = 20% growth rate.

| Level | Growth Rate | State |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | 0–10% | Inflationary Growth |
| L2 | 11–20% | Solid Growth |
| L3 | 21–49% | Breakout Growth |
| L4 | 50%+ | **Hypergrowth** |

> **Only 4% of companies ever generate $10M+ profits.**

## Why Most Don't Get There

> The market leader becomes **the [[Obvious Choice]]**, and enjoys **automatic profits.**

When you become the obvious choice, users and customers recommend your product to friends and colleagues. That's the goal.

## How the [[ProductLed System]] Drives Durable Growth

Durable Growth is the **compounding result** of:
- [[Effortless ARR]] → users upgrade on their own (lower CAC)
- [[Lean Scale]] → product does heavy lifting (lower OpEx per dollar)
- Becoming the [[Obvious Choice]] → automatic referrals (zero CAC for word-of-mouth)
- [[Generosity Moat|Generous free products]] → moat that gets taller every year

All of these layer on top of each other → profit margins improve year over year.

## The Compounding Engine

> Better onboarding → more users reach value → more upgrades → more revenue to reinvest in the product experience → better onboarding…

This is the same loop that drives [[Effortless ARR]]. But extended over years, the *profit* compound is what makes growth durable.

## The Inverse: What Kills Durable Growth

- Cutting marketing/product to harvest short-term profit
- Failing to evolve with the market
- Losing leverage (over-hiring instead of investing in product)
- Letting the [[Obvious Choice]] position slip to a competitor
